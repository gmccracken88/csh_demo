Frequently asked questions

Answers about membership, awards, elections and meetings.

General

What are the benefits of becoming a member?

Discounted meeting registration, eligibility for awards, access to the newsletter, and a vote in elections and at the business meeting. See member benefits.

What forms of payment does CSH accept?

Credit card through Stripe when you register or join online, or a check made payable to Colorado Society of Histotechnology.

How much does it cost to post a job on the CSH website?

Nothing. Histology job openings in Colorado are posted free of charge for up to 90 days. See jobs and resumes.

How much does it cost to post a resume?

Nothing. Resumes are posted free of charge for up to 90 days.

Where can I find the CSH newsletters?

Members can download current and past newsletters from the Newsletters folder in Documents after logging in.

How do I reset my login password?

Use the “Forgot my user name or password” link on the member login page.

Awards

Who can submit nominations for awards?

A CSH member in good standing (dues paid) or any non-member can nominate. The nominator must submit a brief letter of support.

Who is eligible to receive an award?

An active CSH member in good standing. The New Kid on the Block Award and Vendor of the Year Award do not require membership. See the award criteria.

Can members nominate themselves for an award?

Yes, but a member cannot self-nominate for more than one award.

Elections

When are officer elections held?

Every two years. Voting is by secret ballot before the annual meeting of odd-numbered years.

Who makes up the CSH board of directors?

The four officers (President, Vice-President, Secretary and Treasurer) plus a fifth member, normally an immediate past officer. See officers and board.

Who can vote?

Regular members in good standing can vote in officer elections, for awards and at the business meeting.

Meetings

How often does CSH have meetings?

The bylaws call for at least one meeting of the society each calendar year. See upcoming meetings.

Is CSH still using PayPal?

Not through the website. Online payments moved to Stripe in late 2024. A direct PayPal payment link is available on request when a transaction is over the $1,500 credit card limit.

Where can I find the meeting minutes?

Members can find them in the Meeting Minutes folder in Documents after logging in.

Where can I find meeting photo albums?

On the Photos page.

Where can I find meeting presentation handouts?

Members can find them in the Presentations folder in Documents after logging in.
