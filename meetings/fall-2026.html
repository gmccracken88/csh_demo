2026 CSH Fall Meeting

November 13 and 14 at the Translational Medicine Institute, Colorado State University, Fort Collins.

Welcome, histology professionals. This annual two-day meeting brings together histology and pathology professionals from across the state for education, networking and socializing.

Fees

A single $50 registration fee covers all events over both days.

Hotels

CSH is not reserving hotel rooms for this event. There are a number of hotels near the venue.

Credit card. CSH uses Stripe to process online payments. You can pay by credit card when you register.

Vendors and exhibitors

Vendor registration is open for both days of the meeting. For questions, contact our Exhibitor Chairperson, Elizabeth Chlipala, at liz@premierlab.com.
