Our mission

Why the society exists and who it serves.

The Colorado Society of Histotechnology is a non-profit organization designed to unite the histologic technicians and technologists in the State of Colorado by promoting the aims and ideals of histotechnology as well as higher standards in clinical methods and research.

Furthermore, the society promotes mutual understanding between histologic technicians, technologists, physicians, research scientists and all others who are employed in the interest of individual or public health.
