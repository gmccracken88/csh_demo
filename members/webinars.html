CSH Webinar 1, recorded 2/27/21

Documenting Your Immunohistochemical Stain (IHC) Protocol Development and Validation

A review of IHC protocol development and validation, with a focus on how to record and document the steps a laboratory takes when developing a new IHC protocol or testing new lots of antisera from an already validated protocol. Also covers how whole slide imaging can add value to this process.

CSH Webinar 2, recorded 6/5/21

Reversing Historical Trends in Diversity within the US Pathology Workforce

A look at persistent disparities in the representation of minorities and women in pathology across the career pipeline, and how few similar studies exist for other laboratory professionals.
