Member benefits

What you get as a member of CSH.

Discounted meeting registration

  • Regular members of CSH receive a discounted meeting registration rate of $25.00 per class, a savings of 50% compared to the non-member rate of $50.00 per class.
  • Student members of CSH receive a discounted meeting registration rate of $15.00 per class, a 70% savings compared to the non-member rate.

Awards

  • Members can be nominated for awards presented at the annual meeting. Several of these awards carry a monetary value ranging from $200.00 to $500.00.
  • Members can cast votes for awards.
  • Non-members are not eligible for nomination and cannot vote.

Newsletter

  • Members can download current and past copies of the CSH newsletter.
  • Non-members do not have access to the newsletter.

Officer elections

  • Members are able to run for office: president, vice president, treasurer or secretary.
  • Members can cast votes during officer elections, which occur every two years.
  • Non-members are not eligible to run for office or vote in officer elections.

Voting

  • Members can vote during the annual business meeting on agenda items that require a vote of the membership, such as changes to the bylaws.
  • Members can vote for the CSH awards and in officer elections.
  • Non-members are not eligible to vote at the business meeting, for awards or in officer elections.
